# AgentBridge — Demo Video Script

**Target length:** 2–3 minutes
**Format:** Screen recording + voiceover (optionally face cam in corner)

---

## INTRO (0:00–0:15)

**[SCREEN: Cover graphic or hero shot of AgentBridge]**

**VOICEOVER:**
> "Online shopping was designed for humans. But what happens when an AI agent needs to shop for you? Today, agents rely on brittle browser automation and visual scraping. We built something better."

---

## THE PROBLEM (0:15–0:35)

**[SCREEN: Split-screen. Left: an agent struggling with a delivery app — clicking, scrolling, selectors breaking. Right: a simple chat bubble saying "Hey Agent, get me lunch."]**

**VOICEOVER:**
> "Imagine you want to order lunch. Normally, you'd open an app, scroll through restaurants, compare prices, customize your order, and check out. That's fine for a human — but for an AI agent, every click is a fragile automation step that can break at any time."

---

## INTRODUCING AGENTBRIDGE (0:35–0:55)

**[SCREEN: AgentBridge logo + architecture diagram fading in]**

**VOICEOVER:**
> "AgentBridge is an agent-native commerce platform. Instead of forcing agents to pretend to be humans on websites, we give them structured MCP capabilities — semantic actions like search meals, compare prices, and create order."

---

## LIVE DEMO (0:55–2:10)

**[SCREEN: Live demo of the voice agent in action]**

### Part 1 — The Request (0:55–1:10)

**USER (speaking aloud):**
> "Hey Agent, please get me lunch."

**AGENT responds conversationally:**
> "Sure! What kind of food are you in the mood for?"

**USER:**
> "Something healthy — maybe a poke bowl."

**AGENT:**
> "Great choice. What's your budget, and would you like delivery or pickup?"

**USER:**
> "Under twenty dollars, delivery."

### Part 2 — Discovery via AgentBridge (1:10–1:35)

**[SCREEN: Show the agent calling MCP tools — search_meals, filter by budget, compare options. Highlight the structured API calls on screen.]**

**VOICEOVER:**
> "Behind the scenes, the agent is using AgentBridge's MCP layer to search for poke bowls under twenty dollars. No screen scraping, no brittle selectors — just clean, structured API calls."

**AGENT presents options:**
> "I found three options. The Aloha Bowl at fourteen ninety-nine looks great and has strong reviews."

### Part 3 — Human Approval (1:35–1:55)

**USER:**
> "The Aloha Bowl sounds perfect."

**AGENT:**
> "That's fourteen ninety-nine plus two ninety-nine delivery. Shall I place the order?"

**USER:**
> "Go for it."

**AGENT:**
> "Done! Your order has been placed. Estimated delivery in 30 minutes."

### Part 4 — Recap (1:55–2:10)

**[SCREEN: Quick replay of the full flow as a diagram: Human → Voice Agent → AgentBridge (MCP) → Online Store → Order Confirmed]**

**VOICEOVER:**
> "One simple conversation. The agent understood intent, discovered options through AgentBridge, and the user approved the final decision. No apps. No browsing. No friction."

## PRODUCTION NOTES

- **Music:** Low-key ambient/electronic. Something modern and clean — not dramatic.
- **Transitions:** Simple cuts or cross-fades. Avoid flashy motion graphics.
- **Key moments to emphasize:** The simplicity of the voice interaction, the MCP layer calls (show them on screen briefly), and the human approval step.
- **Suggested tools:** OBS or Screen Studio for recording, Descript or CapCut for editing.
- **Thumbnail:** Use the cover graphic (01-cover.svg exported to PNG).
